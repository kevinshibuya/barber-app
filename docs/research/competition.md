# Competition: AI haircut previews (2026-10-22)

MVP feature: the barber photographs the client in the shop, and the app shows AI previews of different cuts.

## Direct: built for barbers
| Product | What it does | Market | Traction | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| StyleForge | One photo becomes a 3D head; 500+ styles; client profiles and style history | Italy first, solo developer | Product Hunt launch Jul 2025; no ratings found | Closest to our concept. Not found in the US, IT, BR or GB App Store on 2026-10-22, so it may be delisted |
| BarberBlend AI | Selfie plus a chosen style gives a result; for barbers and clients | Web/SaaS, $9.99 and $29.99/mo | 9 registered users; launched 2026-01-07 | Tiny |

## Consumer try-on (indirect)
- FadeLogic (ELYON AI): 1 rating, v2.9.3 2026-07-17, $5.99/wk to $99.99/yr. Verified via the iTunes API.
- Fadeup: 5 ratings, v2.6 2026-09-08. Verified via the iTunes API.
- YouCam Makeup (Perfect Corp): about 215K iOS ratings; hairstyle is one feature of a makeup app.
- Plus many low-traction apps and web tools: TryHair.ai, AI Hairstyle TryOn, BarberGPT, Fotor, Facetune, Cutify, MenHair.
- People also use ChatGPT and Gemini with prompts that change only the hair. No evidence found that clients bring these previews to barbers.

## Incumbent platforms
- No haircut preview found at Squire, Booksy, Fresha, Vagaro, GlossGenius, Mangomint, Boulevard, Belasis, AppBarber, Trinks or Simples Agenda. Searches were secondhand; theCut, Square, StyleSeat, Phorest, Zenoti and Avec were not searched.
- Their AI work is front-desk automation: Squire AI (phone, booking, marketing), Fresha AI Concierge ($99.95/location/mo add-on) and Vagaro text tools.
- Perfect Corp sells a white-label haircut simulator that salons can embed in a booking flow, so an incumbent could license it instead of building one.

## Unverified
- How good any of these apps is on fades, tapers and textured or Afro hair. No hands-on tests or reviews were found.
- Google Play, Reddit r/barber, and the incumbents' APIs and integrations.
