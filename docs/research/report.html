Barber App Research

A barber photographs the client in the chair and the app shows AI previews of different cuts on that client. That is the MVP. Later it grows into a barbershop CRM: bookings, client history, retention.

Updated 2026-10-22Name undecidedScope competition + naming

Summary

  • The space is taken, but nobody owns it. Several apps already turn a client photo into AI haircut previews. The barber-first ones are tiny: one solo developer, one with 9 users, one Brazilian launch.
  • Consumer try-on is crowded and mostly unused. FadeLogic has 1 App Store rating and Fadeup has 5. YouCam is the only big one, and hair is a side feature of a makeup app.
  • Booking platforms don't have it yet. Squire, Booksy, Fresha, Vagaro, GlossGenius and the Brazilian tools spend their AI effort on the front desk. Perfect Corp sells an embeddable haircut simulator, so any of them could add previews within months.
  • No one has shown quality on fades or textured hair. No hands-on tests or reviews turned up. That is either the opening or a problem every app shares.

What it means for the MVP

The preview alone won't stay unique. The defensible part is what surrounds it: a fast in-chair flow, notes a barber can actually cut from (guard numbers, fade height, lineup shape), and before-and-after photos saved to the client's history. That history is what the CRM grows from, and an incumbent can't license it from Perfect Corp.

Next steps

Decide how to position against Squire and Perfect Corp, and whether to launch in Brazil or the US first.
